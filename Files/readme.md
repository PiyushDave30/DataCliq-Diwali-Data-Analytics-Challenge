# 🪔 Diwali Sales Pulse 2022–2025
### DataCliq – Skills Wali Diwali Data Analysis Challenge 2026

## 📌 Project Overview
Diwali Sales Pulse is a multi-page Power BI dashboard created as part of the **DataCliq – Skills Wali Diwali Data Analysis Challenge 2026**.

This project analyses Diwali sales data from 2022 to 2025 to understand sales trends, product performance, customer behaviour, regional contributions, and revenue leakage.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyse sales trends across four Diwali seasons.
- Identify top-performing product categories and regions.
- Understand customer purchasing behaviour.
- Evaluate campaign and discount performance.
- Analyse cancellations, returns, and revenue leakage.
- Provide actionable business recommendations.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualisation
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and calculations
- **Excel** – Dataset exploration

## 📊 Dashboard Pages
1. **Executive Overview** – Overall sales performance and key business metrics.
2. **Diwali Countdown** – Sales trends before and around Diwali.
3. **Products & Campaigns** – Product category and campaign performance.
4. **Customers & Regions** – Customer segments, sales channels, and regional analysis.
5. **Operations** – Order cancellations, returns, delivery performance, and revenue leakage.

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Sweets contributed the largest share of sales.
- The North region had the highest regional contribution.
- Cancellations and returns resulted in approximately 16% revenue leakage.
- Discounts and campaign performance varied across product categories.
- Delivery performance and shipping costs provided opportunities for operational improvement.
